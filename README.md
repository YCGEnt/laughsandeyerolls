# Laughs & Eye Rolls

### Adventures with Ms. Murphy

A cinematic digital family archive dedicated to preserving memories, humor, travel adventures, stories, and legacy with personality intact.

"Preserving not just events, but essence." 

---

## Project Vision

Laughs & Eye Rolls exists to preserve family history, humor, personality, and shared experiences in a way that remains meaningful for future generations. The project is designed as a living digital archive where stories, photos, videos, voice notes, travel memories, milestones, and family traditions remain accessible long after the original moments have passed. 

This is not:

* A family blog
* A memorial website
* A scrapbook site
* A social network

This is:

* A luxury digital scrapbook
* A cinematic storytelling experience
* A family archive
* A living legacy platform
* A personality preservation project

---

## Core Design Principles

The site should always feel:

* Warm
* Sophisticated
* Editorial
* Personal
* Memory-rich
* Slightly playful

Avoid:

* Funeral-home aesthetics
* Scrapbook clutter
* Corporate design
* Cartoon graphics
* Excessive sentimentality

The goal is emotional authenticity, not nostalgia for nostalgia's sake.

## Content Philosophy

We preserve:

* Stories
* Humor
* Voice
* Personality
* Traditions
* Travel memories
* Family commentary
* Eye rolls

We do not focus solely on:

* Dates
* Achievements
* Milestones

The archive should answer:

> What did this person feel like?

Not simply:

> What happened?

This principle comes directly from the project's founding purpose of preserving personality, humor, voice, and emotional texture rather than just photos and timelines.

## Development Standards

### Animations

Use:

* Gentle fades
* Soft reveals
* Subtle parallax
* Smooth hover states

Avoid:

* Bounce animations
* Flashing effects
* Aggressive motion
* Excessive scroll hijacking

## Accessibility

## Guiding Principle

If a feature is technically impressive but does not help preserve memories, personality, humor, connection, or storytelling, it probably doesn't belong here.

The archive exists to preserve the moments that make people say:

"That is SO Ms. Murphy."
